🏥 About Sri J.J.R. Physiotherapy Clinic

Sri J.J.R. Physiotherapy Clinic is a trusted healthcare facility located at Marripalem, 7/10th Nagar, Visakhapatnam. Under the expert leadership of Dr. S. Gayathri, the clinic provides comprehensive physiotherapy treatment with a patient-first, evidence-based approach.

The clinic is fully licensed and registered (Reg. No. L-52781) and treats patients across all age groups — from children with developmental disorders to elderly patients with neurological conditions.

🎓 Qualifications & Training

  • BPT — Bachelor of Physiotherapy: Core clinical physiotherapy foundation covering all major systems.
  • M.A.P — Master in Allied Physiotherapy: Advanced training in allied and complementary physiotherapy practices.
  • MPT (Ortho) — Master of Physiotherapy in Orthopaedics: Specialist postgraduate qualification in orthopaedic rehabilitation.
  • Reg. No. L-52781 — Fully licensed and registered with the appropriate physiotherapy council.

🎯 Areas of Specialization

  • Neurological Rehabilitation — Paralysis, Stroke, Parkinson's, G.B. Syndrome, Facial Palsy
  • Orthopaedic Physiotherapy — Joint pain, arthritis, frozen shoulder, sports injuries
  • Spinal Physiotherapy — Cervical spondylosis, lower back pain, sciatica, disc problems
  • Paediatric Physiotherapy — Cerebral palsy, developmental delays, brachial plexus

📋 Our Clinical Approach

Every patient at Sri J.J.R. Physiotherapy Clinic receives a thorough initial assessment before any treatment begins. Dr. Gayathri takes time to understand each patient's history, current condition, limitations and personal goals.

Treatment plans are personalized, progressive and regularly reviewed to ensure optimal recovery outcomes. We combine hands-on therapy, patient education and home exercise programs for holistic rehabilitation.
